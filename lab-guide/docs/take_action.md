## Q11 — Synthesis (Meeting Brief Generation)

!!! action "Generate a meeting brief"

    ```text
    Role: You are a Category Analyst preparing a buyer meeting brief for the Q4 Kitchen Range Review.

    Task: Using the findings from this conversation, produce a meeting brief structured as follows.

    Section 1 — Executive Summary
    A 3-sentence overview of the two key issues: the Apex supply disruption and the ProBlend 9000 growth opportunity.

    Section 2 — Apex Supply Disruption
    Summarise the impact: total revenue loss, which products were most affected, and which stores felt it most. Include the recommended action (activate backup supplier vs. wait for September 8 resolution) with supporting data.

    Section 3 — ProBlend 9000 Growth Case
    Show the revenue trajectory, how it compares to the rest of the blender range, and whether it is cannibalizing existing products. Recommend whether to increase the Q4 buy allocation.

    Section 4 — Decisions Required
    A numbered list of the specific decisions the buyer needs to make in this meeting.

    Output format: Use bold headings for each section, bullet points for supporting data, and keep the total brief under one page.
    ```

**What to expect:** A structured brief with four clear sections — executive summary, supply disruption impact, growth case, and decisions required. CoWork synthesizes everything from the conversation — including the Deep Research report — into a presentation-ready format. Notice you didn't need to re-state any facts; it pulled from all prior answers in the thread.

**Prompting tip — Be prescriptive:** Compare this prompt to the simple questions we asked earlier. When you need a specific output format, tell CoWork exactly what sections you want, what data to include, and how to format it. The more structure you give, the more controlled the output.
